General Ticket Purchasing Guides

Read our step-by-step guides on purchasing tickets safely and avoiding common market risks.

Safety First

How to Book Online Tickets

Purchasing tickets online requires finding a reputable ticket seller, registering a secure user account, selecting verified seating options from interactive maps, and checking out using encrypted credit card methods or payment platforms. Always avoid unverified wire transfers or direct payment links.

Checklist

Before You Buy, Check These...

  • Seat Disclosures: Check if the ticket lists obstructions, general admission zones, or age limits.
  • Delivery Formats: Understand if the tickets are mobile transfer, printed PDF, or physical courier items.
  • Refund Policies: Ensure the vendor has a refund guarantee in case the event gets postponed or canceled.
Fraud Warning

How to Avoid Ticket Scams

To avoid counterfeit listings, never purchase tickets via social media posts, direct messaging, or wire services. Always make sure the tickets use dynamic barcodes (which rotate constantly to protect security). If a seller pushes for payment through gift cards or cash-sharing apps, abort the transaction immediately.
